How ordering works

Three steps from your files to your customer

  1. 1 Build the order Add items one at a time, drop a folder of files and set them up in bulk, or paste rows from a spreadsheet and we match the images by file name.
  2. 2 We check every file Resolution, bleed and size are checked as you build. We look again before printing, and send a proof first when you ask for one.
  3. 3 We print and ship Send it all to one address, split it across your customer's locations, or pick it up in Arlington. Tracking comes by email.
Sheet builder

Drop all your artwork for signs, stickers or DTF. Set each design's size and quantity, and we lay it out on full sheets. If a sheet has empty space, we show you how many more fit at no extra cost.
